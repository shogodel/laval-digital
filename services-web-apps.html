— Questions

Frequently Asked

How much does a website cost?

It depends on scope — pages, features, integrations. After a free 30-minute discovery call you receive a fixed, itemised quote within 48 hours. No hourly black box.

How long does it take?

A showcase site typically ships in 4–8 weeks; platforms and apps run in short sprints with a demo at each step, so you see progress instead of waiting for a big reveal.

Will I own my site and my data?

Yes — code, content, accounts and data are 100% yours, documented and handed over. No lock-in, no hostage situations.

Do you redesign existing sites?

Yes. We start with an audit of what converts and what leaks, keep what works, and rebuild the rest — design, speed, SEO and measurement included.

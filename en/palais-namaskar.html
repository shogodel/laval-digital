Palais Namaskar: the digital jewel case of a palace.

How do you translate the refinement of an exceptional resort online? For Palais Namaskar, we designed an immersive showcase website that extends the experience of the place itself and turns a visit into genuine booking intent.

A palace, an uncompromising standard.

Tucked at the gates of Marrakech, facing the Atlas Mountains, Palais Namaskar is a luxury resort where every detail matters. A palace, private villas with pools, a spa, fine-dining tables: this is the kind of address guests choose for an experience, not merely a room. Its website needed to live up to that promise — a showcase capable of conveying the emotion of the place to a demanding international clientele before they even arrive.

The existing site did not do the property justice: dated navigation, under-used photography, scattered content and a booking journey that was hard to follow. In a market where travellers compare several addresses within minutes, that friction is costly. The stakes were never purely aesthetic — the goal was to turn admiration into genuine intent, then into a confirmed booking.

We approached the brief as a brand-experience project as much as a web development one, working closely with the hotel's teams to understand their guests, their peak seasons and their distribution channels.

The challenge at hand.

Three challenges stacked up. First, showcasing already-rich photography without ever weighing down load times — because on mobile, every lost second is a lost visitor. Second, structuring a complex offer (rooms, villas, spa, dining, seminars and private events) with total clarity, so every type of guest finds what they need immediately.

Finally, smoothing the path to booking without disrupting the hushed atmosphere of the place with overly aggressive calls to action. Everything had to stay perfectly consistent in both French and English, and reflect a premium brand identity on every page, from the largest screen down to a smartphone.

Our tailored response.

We rebuilt the information architecture around emotion: an immersive homepage that sets the tone within seconds, sections structured like chapters in a story, and full-screen photography given room to breathe. The art direction plays on the contrast between generous negative space and warm visuals, true to the spirit of the place — a luxury that breathes rather than one that shouts.

On the technical side, images are served in modern formats and multiple resolutions, with carefully managed lazy loading, all overseen by our Websites & Platforms practice. The booking journey was shortened and made obvious from every page, drawing on the best practice of our UX/UI team: a persistent booking button, key information always within reach, and fewer steps before confirmation.

  • Art direction and full interface redesign
  • Information architecture and a shortened booking journey
  • Performance and image optimisation (modern formats, srcset)
  • Consistent, search-optimised bilingual FR / EN version

The levers we used.

The project brought together several in-house disciplines: interface design, high-performance front-end development, technical SEO optimisation and integration with the hotel's existing booking tools. That cross-disciplinary approach is our trademark: rather than stacking up separate providers, the client gets a single point of contact who orchestrates design, code and visibility.

We also took care over the invisible but decisive foundations: structured data for search engines, clean markup, accessibility and mobile compatibility. Taken together, these are what separate a beautiful website from one that genuinely works for the property.

What we take away.

On a premium project, performance is itself part of the luxury: a slow website betrays the promise. Getting the balance right between visual richness and speed proved decisive, and it confirms a conviction we apply to every mandate — aesthetics and performance are not opposites, they feed each other.

This project also illustrates the value of an ongoing dialogue with the client: the best architectural decisions came from a detailed understanding of their guests. Want a website that matches the standard of your property? Talk to our team in Marrakech, or explore our other projects.

— Our method

From sketch
to a live website.

A process refined across more than 820 projects, most delivered on the first submitted budget. Nothing at launch is a surprise — every screen, delay and change of scope was flagged while it was still cheap to fix.

01

Brief & Discovery (Week 1)

A framing workshop brings every stakeholder into one room or one call. Objectives, technical constraints, target users, budget and calendar get written down, and competitors get benchmarked properly. What comes out is a shared brief everyone signs off on, not a summary nobody read.

2–4h workshopCompetitive benchmarkApproved brief
02

UX & Architecture (Weeks 2–3)

Sitemap, user flows and low-fidelity wireframes come first, and no colour is applied until the logic holds up under questioning. It's deliberate: fixing a wireframe takes ten minutes; fixing the same mistake once it's coded costs closer to two hours.

Wireframes in Figma2–3 feedback roundsClient sign-off
03

UI Design & Prototype (Weeks 3–5)

High-fidelity mockups, a full design system and a clickable prototype: you experience your future website before a single line of production code exists. Feedback here costs nothing; once development starts, changes of direction are billed by the hour, and we say so upfront.

HF mockupsDesign systemFigma prototype
04

Sprint-Based Development (Weeks 5–N)

Two-week sprints, each closing with a working staging environment rather than a status slide. You test, comment and approve directly inside Notion or Linear, never buried in email. Automated tests and a systematic code review gate every merge, so speed never means a broken build reaching staging.

Staging env. each sprintAutomated testsSystematic code review
05

QA, Launch & Handover

Cross-browser testing, accessibility and performance audits, a written user manual, CMS training where relevant, content migration and a zero-downtime release. One month of free support follows launch, because the questions that matter tend to surface once real users start clicking around.

Lighthouse auditTraining included1 month post-launch support

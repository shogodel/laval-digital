A budget that burns, clicks that don't convert.

Plenty of advertisers open a Google Ads account, push a handful of keywords, switch the budget on… and watch the money evaporate without knowing what's actually paying off. Mis-set bids, irrelevant queries left unexcluded, generic ads, a landing page that drives people away: every weak link burns clicks that were paid for nothing. Paid search isn't a switch you flip — it's precision engineering.

Paid search has one unbeatable advantage over organic: immediacy. From day one, you capture demand that's already been expressed — people typing exactly what you sell. But that only works if campaigns are structured by intent, ads are written to earn the click, conversion tracking is wired up properly, and traffic lands on a page built to convert. Skip any of that and you're paying Google to fatten your competitors.

Unlike organic search work, which builds a durable asset, Google Ads buys instant visibility that we steer by the numbers — part of our wider digital marketing agency in Marrakech, so every dirham spent serves one coherent acquisition strategy, not an isolated expense.

What Google Ads really delivers.

Piloted well, paid search becomes an acquisition tap you open and close at will: you invest, you measure, you scale what works. Unlike SEO, which builds over time, Google Ads delivers results from the first week — ideal for a launch, a promotion, or an urgent need for leads.

  • Leads and sales from day one, without waiting for organic rankings.
  • Full budget control: you decide how much to invest, when, and on what.
  • Measurement down to the dirham of cost per lead and return on investment.
  • Immediate presence at the top of Google on your most profitable queries.
  • A fast testing lever to validate a market, an offer or a message.

Your deliverables, at every stage

You keep full control and visibility over every decision. In practice, managing your account with our PPC team gives you:

  • A structured account plan: campaigns, ad groups, keywords and exclusions.
  • Complete conversion tracking setup and measurement goals.
  • Ads and extensions written, tested and continuously optimised.
  • Landing page recommendations to improve conversion rate.
  • Monthly business-focused reporting: cost per lead, ROAS and next steps.

Palais Namaskar: winning a palace's trust.

Palais Namaskar is a luxury resort on the outskirts of Marrakech. This case study charts our collaboration: translating the standards of an exceptional address online and turning admiration into bookings.

A similar project?Website design
ContentsThe clientThe missionResultsGet in touch

The client.

Tucked away on the outskirts of Marrakech, Palais Namaskar belongs to the very exclusive circle of exceptional resorts. Private villas, a spa, fine-dining tables and water gardens: every detail answers to an international clientele used to the very best. In this market, the first impression is made online, long before the guest arrives, at the precise moment they compare a handful of exceptional addresses.

The property turned to 212 Communication for a showcase worthy of its standing. Since 2007, our Marrakech agency has supported the region's high-end brands, and that closeness to the local luxury world weighed in the decision. Our role: bring the emotion of the place to the screen, with the same rigour expected in a palace. This partnership illustrates our culture of premium web support.

The mission.

The mission covered a complete redesign of the showcase website and its booking journey. We had to elevate an already rich set of photography without weighing the pages down, structure a complex offer — rooms, villas, spa, dining, events — and make booking obvious from every screen, in both French and English. The brief also meant keeping a consistent brand identity from page to page.

We mobilised our Sites & Platforms practice around a high-end art direction and high-performance build. Full-screen photography, generous negative space and a shortened path to booking guided our choices, with constant attention paid to load times. The detail of the build is presented in the Palais Namaskar project, which documents the design decisions and the information architecture chosen.

This case study sits alongside a series of collaborations with demanding brands, visible among our client case studies and our project portfolio.

What we take from it.

On a luxury project, performance is part of the experience: a slow site betrays the promise of the place. The balance found between visual richness and speed was decisive, as was brand consistency across both languages. The result reinforces a simple conviction — aesthetics and efficiency strengthen each other rather than compete.

This collaboration sums up how we work with luxury hospitality: listening to the place, demanding finish and attention to the smallest detail. It's also a reminder that a premium site is never finished — it lives with the seasons, the offers and the campaigns, which calls for a partner able to support the property over the long run rather than for the length of a single delivery.

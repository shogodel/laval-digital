Sol Oasis a residence that tells its own story.

How do you bring a residence to life on social media, beyond a simple property listing? For Sol Oasis, we rolled out community management that creates desire and builds a community, backed by a coherent social media strategy.

A residence, a way of life.

Sol Oasis isn't just a residential development: it's a way of living, a certain light, a manner of inhabiting Marrakech. But that extra something, the spark that triggers the coup de foudre, can't be conveyed by a spec sheet or a list of amenities. It has to be told, in images and stories, wherever attention now lives — on social media, in the daily feed of people dreaming of somewhere else and of a certain art of living.

Before our involvement, the residence's social presence stayed occasional and purely promotional: a handful of property photos, little regularity, no real community. The posts read like listings, never like a story. The challenge was to move from a listing logic to a brand logic — establishing a presence that's desirable, consistent and embodied, one that makes people want to belong to the place long before they ever walk through its door. It was about creating desire, not merely informing. An upscale residence isn't chosen for its amenities alone, but for the projection it allows: picturing yourself living there, imagining your mornings, your gatherings, your quiet moments. It's that story that social media, handled well, tells better than any other medium.

The challenge we faced.

The first challenge was editorial: finding the right tone between real-estate prestige and the closeness social media demands. Too institutional, and the message bores people away; too light, and it devalues the residence and blurs its positioning. We needed an editorial line able to sell a way of life without ever looking like a plain sales brochure — a recognisable voice that inspires confidence while staying warm and approachable.

The second challenge was consistency. A community isn't built in a handful of posts, but through a steady rhythm, quality content and attentive engagement with conversations. Without a sustained cadence, without quick replies to messages, without a presence in the comments, even the best strategy fades for lack of upkeep. The goal, then, was a presence that felt alive, credible and lasting, capable not only of charming people but also of genuinely feeding the residence's sales pipeline. In other words, posting beautiful images wasn't enough: we had to build a presence that works, day after day, to generate qualified leads and to nurture the place's reputation.

Our tailored response.

We defined a clear editorial line that showcases Sol Oasis's way of life: light, spaces, architectural details and moments of everyday living rather than plain property shots. This direction is carried day to day by our community management team, with a regular editorial calendar, recurring segments and formats designed for each platform. Nothing is left to chance or improvised at the last minute: every post serves the overall story.

Visual production was crafted in keeping with the brand's spirit, aligned with a social media strategy built for engagement rather than visibility alone. We run the community through every interaction — replies to comments, private messages, moderation — turning an audience into a relationship, and that relationship into concrete opportunities for the residence. A follower who feels heard and understood becomes an advocate, then, often, a qualified lead. We pay particular attention to response time and tone: on social media, the responsiveness and accuracy of an exchange often say more about a residence than the most polished visual ever could.

  • Editorial line and brand positioning
  • Polished social media content creation
  • Editorial calendar and regular posting
  • Community engagement and moderation

The levers we used.

The project brought together editorial strategy, content creation and community management within a single coordinated set-up, rather than one split into silos. This continuity, in keeping with our social media expertise, ensures a consistent presence from one post to the next and avoids the pitfall of posts with no common thread, which dilute the message rather than reinforce it. Regularity and consistency are the real engines of performance here. Every piece of content sits within an overall plan, with its highlights and its recurring appointments, rather than being published whenever time allows: it's that planning which turns a sum of posts into a genuine brand presence.

We also thought through the bridges to acquisition: content linked to a possible web presence and consistency with the residence's other touchpoints. Everyone won over by the content should be able, in a few clicks, to move from interest to first contact, with no break or friction. Social media opens the relationship; it still has to offer a clear path towards conversion, and that's what we built.

What we took away.

In upscale real estate, social media doesn't sell square metres: it sells a longing, an imagined life, a sense of belonging. Desire is built over time, through consistency and the right tone, never through blunt promotion that wears people out and drives them away. This project illustrates our approach — an engaged community, even a modest one, is worth infinitely more than a large but indifferent or bought audience. It's genuine interactions, spontaneous messages and sincere shares that end up turning a residence into a coveted address.

We also took away that the value of a social presence is measured over the long run: it's consistency and relationship that ultimately pay off. Do you have a residence, a place or a brand to bring to life on social media? Browse our other work or talk to our team.

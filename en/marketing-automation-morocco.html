Marketing automation Morocco: contacts converted on autopilot

Not every prospect is ready to buy today — but most will be tomorrow, provided you stay present at the right moment. Marketing automation Morocco puts your customer relationship on autopilot: scenarios that welcome, follow up and convert your contacts with no manual work, so a lead never gets left to go cold again.

Leads collected, then forgotten.

Every day, new contacts enter your database: a form filled in, a guide downloaded, a cart abandoned. And every day, most of them go cold because nobody has time to follow up with each one individually. It's a silent leak: you pay to generate leads through your campaigns, then let them evaporate for lack of follow-up. The money invested in acquisition goes up in smoke at the very last step.

Marketing automation solves this by automating the relationship. A new contact receives a welcome sequence; an abandoned cart triggers a reminder; a lukewarm prospect is nurtured with useful content until they're ready; an existing customer receives relevant offers at the right time. Everything fires based on each person's behaviour, at scale, without you having to lift a finger once the scenarios are live.

As part of our wider digital marketing agency in Marrakech practice, we connect automation to the rest of your acquisition: leads captured through your Google Ads campaigns or your social channels enter scenarios that nurture them all the way to a sale. Automation isn't an isolated tool — it's the engine that makes everything you invest upstream pay off.

An automation method,
in four stages.

Every automation project follows the same progression, from mapping journeys to continuous optimisation. We automate what actually makes sense, never automation for its own sake.

01 / 04

Journeys & Segmentation

We map your contacts' journeys — from first interaction through to purchase and retention — and identify the key moments where an automatic follow-up changes everything. We then segment your database by behaviour and profile, because a relevant message always starts with good segmentation.

Journey mappingSegmentationTrigger points

02 / 04

Scenarios & Content

Design of the workflows: welcome sequences, nurturing, cart-abandonment reminders, reactivation, upsell. We write the emails and messages for each scenario, in the right tone with clear calls to action, sometimes drafted faster with help from our AI & Data practice and always reviewed to keep a human voice.

Email workflowsLead nurturingCopywriting

03 / 04

CRM & Integrations

Setup or configuration of your automation tool and CRM, connected to your website and your forms. We work alongside our web agency in Marrakech so every lead source feeds the database cleanly and triggers the right scenarios, with no duplicates and no data loss.

CRM configurationWebsite integrationsLead management

04 / 04

Measurement & Optimisation

Once the scenarios are live, we track open rates, click rates and conversion at every stage. We test subject lines, content and send timing, then adjust workflows to improve returns. An automation system is a living thing: it gets more effective the more it's refined with data.

What automation changes in your business.

Well designed, marketing automation works for you around the clock: it welcomes, follows up and converts while your team focuses on higher-value tasks. Every lead captured is used to its full potential, every existing customer is reactivated at the right moment — and your revenue grows without your workload exploding.

  • More conversions from the same leads, thanks to systematic follow-up.
  • Significant time saved: reminders run on autopilot.
  • A personalised customer relationship at scale, based on each person's behaviour.
  • Stronger retention: reactivation, upsell and offers at the right moment.
  • Higher profitability across all your acquisition, since no lead is wasted.

You get a turnkey, documented and scalable system. In concrete terms, a marketing automation Morocco project with Laval Digital gets you:

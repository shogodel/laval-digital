Rently Pro renting, in your pocket.

Rently Pro is a rental management platform. This case study traces our collaboration: designing a mobile app that simplifies everyday life for landlords and property managers, from listing a property to tracking payments.

A similar project?Mobile apps
ContentsThe clientThe missionResultsGet in touch

The client.

Rently Pro is a PropTech platform dedicated to rental management. Its ambition: bring together, in a single tool, listing a property, communicating with tenants and tracking payments. In this market, user experience makes the difference — a confusing app gets uninstalled, no matter how rich its feature set. Property managers already juggle plenty of tools: theirs had to stand out through simplicity rather than a long list of features.

The company turned to 212 Communication to design its mobile app. Our agency builds digital products where actual use comes before technical showmanship, and that philosophy shaped the whole project. Our mission: turn a complex trade into a simple, reliable experience, backed by our expertise in mobile applications.

The mission.

The brief covered the design of the experience and interface, followed by development of the app itself: property management, a rent dashboard, notifications and tenant communication. Every screen had to stay readable despite the depth of features, with an interface non-specialist users could pick up straight away.

We led the UX/UI, the development and the testing, confronting the mockups against property managers' real-world use very early on. That feedback let us refine the user flows before launch and remove the unnecessary steps that weigh down so many business apps. Constant attention went into the reliability of financial data, since on a rent-tracking tool, the smallest display error erodes trust. The detail of the build is presented in the Rently Pro project.

This case study sits alongside other work featured in our client case studies and our project portfolio.

What we take away.

On a business app, simplicity has to be earned: removing friction takes more work than adding features. By focusing the experience on property managers' essential tasks, Rently Pro gained in adoption and satisfaction. The best feature is often the one you never have to explain, and a clear dashboard beats a long user manual.

This partnership illustrates our approach to product design: start from real usage, test early, and ship a tool people open every day without thinking about it. It also shows that a good business app is measured less by its number of features than by the time it saves the people who actually use it. Are you building a PropTech product or a business app?

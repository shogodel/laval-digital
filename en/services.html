— Our method

The way we work.

All our projects follow the same protocol: rigorous scoping, agile execution. Four steps, zero nasty surprises.

Step 01

Diagnosis & scoping

A complimentary two-hour workshop: audit of what exists, clarification of your goals, identification of high-impact levers. The outcome: a jointly built brief.

Step 02

Strategy & architecture

A detailed proposal, transparent pricing, a milestone-based schedule: you sign knowing exactly the deliverable, the deadline and the cost.

Step 03

Sprint-based execution

Deliveries every two weeks: the project takes shape before your eyes, you validate and redirect as you go. The final delivery holds no surprises.

Step 04

Measurement & optimisation

After launch, the KPIs defined in phase one are closely tracked: monthly reports, continuous adjustments. Going live is not the end of the project — it is its starting point.

Web hosting and security Morocco: keeping your site online, protected and fast

A site online is never a given.

It's tempting to assume that once a site is launched, it just runs itself. The reality is harsher: servers that crawl at peak times, an expired SSL certificate throwing up a frightening warning, an outdated plugin that opens a security hole, or a hack that turns your showcase site into a spam page. Every incident costs visitors, rankings and credibility — usually at the worst possible moment, when traffic is at its highest.

The problem is rarely bad luck. It comes from bargain-basement hosting, updates that keep getting pushed back, and no recent backups to fall back on. Without monitoring, an attack or an outage goes unnoticed until a customer tells you the site is down. Without a reliable backup, you can't even roll things back. Security and uptime have to be prepared for in advance — never sorted out in a panic.

We treat hosting as the foundation of your presence online, serving your websites just as much as your applications, and building on the same standards as the rest of our web agency Marrakech practice. Servers sized to your traffic, active protections and managed hosting: a fast, stable site also supports your organic search rankings, since Google rewards sites that are available and performant.

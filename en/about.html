About Laval Digital: a digital agency, four practices

Laval Digital was born in 2007 in a twenty-square-metre room, with three people, one shared computer and a first client who wanted “just a logo”. Nineteen years later we are twenty-five, we have shipped over 820 projects, and the conviction of day one hasn't moved an inch: a brand is built over time, not in a single campaign.

— Who we are

An independent house, run by craftspeople.

Laval Digital is an independent company: no group owns our capital, nobody imposes a tool, a supplier or a quarterly margin target on us. That freedom has a cost — we grow slowly — and a precious upside: we only recommend what we would use ourselves. It is the first thing clients notice, and often what they remember.

Under one roof: strategists, designers, developers, copywriters, community managers and data engineers. They don't work in relay, one after another — they work together, on the same project, from the very first workshop. That is why a website leaves this place with its content plan, its SEO and its measurement already in place, rather than three months later.

— Our story

Four steps,
zero surprises.

An agency is not told through slogans but through decisions. Here are the five that shaped ours, year after year.

01

2007 — Three people, one room, one first client

The Laval Digital adventure began in Marrakech as a design studio: logos, brochures, posters. Clients were restaurant owners in Guéliz, guesthouses in the Medina, a few firms. We listened, we delivered on time, and word of mouth did the rest. First lesson learned: reliability beats noise.

Design studioVisual identityFirst Marrakech clients
02

2012 — The web and social turn

Clients no longer wanted just an image: they wanted an audience. We hired our first in-house developers and community managers and stopped outsourcing the technical side. That costly choice underpins everything since: what we design, we can also build and run.

First in-house developersCommunity managementNo more outsourcing
03

2016 — Europe knocks on the door

A Belgian client trusted Laval Digital with one project, then two, then asked for a dedicated team. Outsourcing became a business line of its own, contractually structured, with intellectual property and GDPR guarantees. It now accounts for nearly a third of our activity and has a page of its own: our digital offshoring offer in Marrakech.

France · Belgium · NetherlandsDedicated teamsGDPR compliance
04

2020 — Proof through data

The pandemic cut budgets and raised a new demand: prove it. We instrumented everything — traffic, cost per acquisition, conversion rate, return on ad spend — and monthly reporting became a deliverable like any other. That culture of numbers now runs through our digital marketing and SEO practice and the rest of the house.

Monthly reportingContractual KPIsCulture of measurement
05

2023 — Artificial intelligence goes into production

While the market was commenting, we were shipping: conversational assistants, automated prospecting, internal workflows, predictive analysis. Our AI practice was born from a refusal of the free demo — every project starts from a measurable use case. It now lives in our AI, data and automation practice, the youngest of the four.

A multidisciplinary team in the heart of Marrakech

Twenty-five people make up Laval Digital today, at the business centre: strategists who ask the awkward questions, art directors who refuse the déjà-vu, developers who meet deadlines, copywriters who write in French and English, community managers who answer on Sundays when they must, and data engineers who turn an unreadable spreadsheet into a decision. The average age is around thirty-two; average tenure exceeds four years, which in our trade is almost an anomaly.

We hire rarely and slowly, favouring curiosity over diplomas. Every newcomer spends their first weeks on real projects, paired with an experienced colleague, and nobody talks to a client without understanding their business. Our teams train continuously — SEO, generative AI, accessibility, security — because digital know-how left untended expires in eighteen months. You will meet these faces from the first workshop: here, the people who sell the project are the people who deliver it.

Our Marrakech roots are not decorative. They give us a fine reading of the ground — Hivernage hospitality, Medina riads, Guéliz retail, the Sidi Ghanem industrial zone — and genuine proximity to our Moroccan clients, while the European time zone makes Laval Digital a partner as available from Paris or Brussels as from Casablanca. Want to know who you would be working with? Come and see us in Marrakech: the coffee is on us, and the conversation commits you to nothing.

Four practices under one roof

A growing company always ends up stacking suppliers: a studio for the logo, an IT firm for the website, a freelancer for social, a consultant for SEO. Each does their job well, nobody answers for the whole. Laval Digital built the opposite — four complementary practices, one invoice, one accountable contact.

  • Web & Apps. Showcase sites, e-commerce, business platforms and mobile apps, built to convert and to hold up under load. The detail lives on our website and application development practice.
  • Marketing & Performance. SEO, Google Ads, social media, brand content and automation, steered by cost per acquisition rather than intuition.
  • AI & Data. Conversational assistants, internal process automation, governed content generation and predictive analysis — the newest practice, and the most requested.
  • Business & Reputation. Strategy, monitoring, community management and crisis handling, with our online reputation and community management practice on the front line when the storm hits.

Depending on your need, one practice leads and the others support. If local visibility is the priority, the natural entry point is our communication agency page for Marrakech; if you are running a full digital transformation, start with our digital approach in Marrakech. And if you would rather judge on evidence, our recent work and the brands that trust us speak better than we do.

How we work, from brief to measurement

The Laval Digital method has four movements and has not changed in ten years — only the tools have. Listen first: a framing workshop brings together your decision-makers and your field teams, because the real friction is never visible from a boardroom. Then recommend: we formalise a positioning, an action plan and a costed timeline, which you approve before a single line of code or copy is written. Produce: creation and development advance in short iterations with regular checkpoints, so that at delivery you discover nothing. Finally, measure: going live is not the end of the project but its beginning — the numbers arrive, and we adjust.

That cycle explains much of our longevity. It makes nasty surprises rare, budgets manageable and results arguable on facts. It also explains why we regularly turn work down: when the timeline or the budget make seriousness impossible, better to say so before starting. If this way of working speaks to you, write us a few lines about your project — we reply within 48 hours, with a recommendation rather than a random price.

Nadjane: elegance that converts.

How do you translate a fashion brand's world into a website that truly sells? For Nadjane, we designed a website that is both desirable and effective, where every page guides the visitor from emotion to purchase, powered by our UX/UI expertise.

A brand, a world to embody.

Nadjane is a brand that lives on aesthetics and desire. Its world — fabrics, colours, silhouettes — is felt instinctively in store, but struggled to carry the same weight online. The existing site looked more like a catalogue than a brand showcase: functional, but soulless and with little power to convert. Visitors found products there, never a story, and left without feeling what makes the label distinctive.

For a retail label, digital is no longer a simple extension of the shop — it is often the first contact, sometimes the only one. A customer discovers the brand on her phone, forms an opinion within seconds, and decides whether to stay or leave. The challenge was therefore twofold: convey Nadjane's identity from the homepage onward and, in the same movement, remove every obstacle standing between a seduced visitor and a confirmed purchase. Seduction alone isn't enough; the momentum has to be carried all the way through. In a sector where the competition is only a swipe away, the smallest hesitation, the smallest delay or the smallest neglected detail can be enough to send a customer to another label. The site therefore had to be both a statement of identity and a conversion machine, without either dimension ever smothering the other.

The challenge at hand.

The first challenge was artistic: showcasing fashion products with the precision of a magazine, without ever sacrificing display speed. A fashion brand is judged in a single glance, and a slow or dull website immediately tarnishes the promise. What was needed was a setting that flatters every piece, stages fabrics and details, while staying light and immediate, even on an average mobile connection.

The second challenge was commercial: turning appeal into conversion. Unengaging product pages, a purchase journey that ran too long, insufficient reassurance on delivery and returns — all leak points that let convinced customers slip away. The goal was for the desire the brand creates to carry through to the basket, then to payment, on mobile first, where most retail traffic happens. Every screen had to bring the visitor closer to purchase, never further away. That's the paradox of online retail: you have to take time to make people dream, then move fast so doubt never has a chance to settle between desire and payment.

Our tailored response.

We rebuilt the site around Nadjane's identity: pared-back art direction, full-format visuals, confident typography and a navigation rhythm that lets products breathe. Every page tells the brand's story before it sells, a deliberate choice worked out with our UX/UI team so that aesthetics always serve usability. Negative space, light and restraint create an immediate impression of prestige, without clutter or artifice.

On the conversion side, we reworked the product pages to make them more desirable and more informative, shortened the purchase journey and strengthened reassurance at every step — delivery, secure payment, returns. The site, built by our Websites & Platforms practice, serves optimised images in modern formats to stay fast despite the visual richness. The result is a desirable website that remains formidably effective: emotion and performance pull in the same direction here. We made sure every interface element — button, price, availability, call to action — is both beautiful and instantly understandable, because visual doubt is always paid for in lost sales.

  • Art direction and digital identity redesign
  • Polished, desire-driven product pages
  • Shortened purchase journey and reassurance
  • Performance and image optimisation

The levers we used.

Beyond design and development, we built in a solid SEO foundation: clear structure, optimised product pages, content designed to capture searches around the brand and its products. A retail website is only worth as much as it is found, then understood, then chosen — and that chain starts well before the homepage, in the search results. So we gave search visibility as much care as aesthetics. A logical category architecture, clean URLs and rich content give the site the best chance of being visible on the queries that matter, without distorting the brand experience.

We also designed the site to work hand in hand with the brand's social media, an essential relay in fashion. Adaptable visuals, shareable pages and a consistent tone with the campaigns run by our social media teams: the site becomes the anchor point for a coherent brand ecosystem. A post inspires, leads back to a product page, which converts — the loop is complete, from first glance to purchase.

What we take away.

In fashion, beauty and performance are not opposites: a desirable website that converts poorly misses its purpose, and an efficient but dull one betrays the brand. The real work lies in reconciling both, on every screen and at every interaction. This project illustrates our approach — emotion opens the sale, the journey closes it, and neither works without the other. A beautiful image attracts, but it is the smoothness of the path to payment that ultimately decides whether the visit becomes an order.

We also take away that a brand website is never fixed: it lives in step with collections and campaigns, and benefits from resting on a foundation built to evolve. Building a brand you want to shine online? Browse our other projects or talk directly with our team.

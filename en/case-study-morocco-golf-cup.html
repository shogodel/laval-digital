Morocco Corporate Golf Cup: taking the event online.

The Morocco Corporate Golf Cup brings executives and partners together around golf. This case study charts our collaboration: giving a corporate event a clear digital showcase and a smooth registration flow.

A similar project?Website design
ContentsThe clientThe missionResultsGet in touch

The client.

The Morocco Corporate Golf Cup is a sporting fixture that brings together executives, companies and partners around golf. A corporate event is judged on its ability to mobilise: clarity of information, visibility for partners and a frictionless sign-up all decide its success. Everything often comes down to the weeks before kick-off, when every partner expects visibility and every participant expects a hassle-free registration. The website becomes the rallying point for the whole organisation.

The organisers called on 212 Communication to build the event's digital showcase. Used to event projects run against a tight calendar, our agency delivered fast without sacrificing finish. For a competition that carries the image of its partner companies, the bar for seriousness was at least as high as on any brand project. Our mission: give the competition a professional, dynamic image, built on an event website designed for action.

The mission.

The mission covered the design of a full event website: presenting the programme, showcasing partners and sponsors, a gallery of past editions and a participant registration journey. All of it had to read well on mobile, load fast and be simple to update as the event approached.

We took charge of the art direction, the build and performance optimisation, with particular attention paid to registration conversion. The update workflow was designed so organisers could enrich the programme and add partners on their own, without depending on a developer for every change. The detail of the build is presented in the Morocco Corporate Golf Cup project, which documents the set-up deployed.

This case study joins other work visible among our client case studies and our project portfolio.

What we take from it.

An event website lives by a tight calendar: it must be ready fast, evolve easily and convert without friction. By focusing the journey on registration and partner visibility, the event gained in both reach and efficiency. Simplicity is the real luxury here, and responsiveness a decisive edge.

This partnership illustrates our event know-how: fast scoping, careful execution and a sense for conversion. It's also a reminder that an event website has only a few weeks to prove its worth, and that everything, from the first screen to the registration button, must converge on the single action expected of the visitor.

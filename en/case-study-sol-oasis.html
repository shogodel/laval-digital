Sol Oasis a residence that shines.

Sol Oasis is an upscale residence in Marrakech. This case study traces our collaboration: giving voice to a residential way of life and nurturing a community of buyers and residents.

A similar project?Community management
ContentsThe clientThe missionResultsGet in touch

The client.

Sol Oasis is an upscale residence designed around a way of life in Marrakech: generous spaces, carefully finished amenities and a peaceful setting. In a competitive property market, a development is judged as much on its image as on its floor plans. The project therefore needed to exist well beyond the brochure and build a lasting bond with both future buyers and residents. Faced with an oversupplied market, a residence that tells no story quickly becomes interchangeable in the eyes of a hurried buyer.

The residence chose 212 Communication to bring this world to life on social media. Our agency knows Marrakech's local market and the codes of a demanding, high-net-worth clientele. Our mission: turn a property development into a desirable brand, with the consistency and the right tone that professional community management requires.

The mission.

The brief was to build a coherent editorial line and a regular publishing calendar. Mood photography, amenities given pride of place, storytelling around the neighbourhood and the way of life: every piece of content had to reinforce the residence's perceived value and drive enquiries, without ever tipping into pure sales talk.

We led the graphic design, the copywriting and the community management, with month-on-month performance tracking. Reading that data let us adjust formats and posting times as the weeks went by, and flag the most promising signals of interest to the sales teams. Moderating messages and handling incoming enquiries were also part of the remit, so that no contact was ever left unanswered. The full approach is detailed in the Sol Oasis project, which presents the content strategy we rolled out.

This collaboration sits alongside other work featured in our client case studies and our project portfolio.

What we take away.

Selling a place to live means selling an emotion: consistency and visual coherence matter as much as any single listing. By establishing a steady presence and a warm tone, the residence gained visibility and credibility with its target audience. Trust is built post by post, rarely in a single campaign.

This partnership illustrates our approach to residential property: a patient narrative, carefully crafted visuals and close attention to the community. It also shows that a qualified audience beats a large one — a few dozen genuinely interested prospects are worth more than thousands of indifferent followers. Do you run a residential development looking for a social presence worthy of it? Let's talk with our team.

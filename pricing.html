Pricing Questions

Are these fixed prices?

The setup fees are fixed and quoted upfront. For phone calls, you will be billed directly by the voice platform at their standard rate (typically $0.13 to $0.31 CAD per minute depending on the voice and LLM you choose). You own your account. No hidden markups.

What is excluded?

Ad spend, software licenses, app subscriptions and premium theme fees are excluded and always listed separately before you sign.

Do I own the code and data?

Yes — code, content, accounts and data are 100% yours. No lock-in, no middlemen.

What if I need more than the package covers?

You get a fixed quote for the extra scope before any work starts. Nothing is ever billed without your written approval.

Can I pause or cancel?

Yes — care plans pause or cancel anytime with one message. No fees, no retention calls.

How do payments work?

Fixed quote first; deposit to schedule, balance at handover. No recurring monthly fees unless you add a care plan.

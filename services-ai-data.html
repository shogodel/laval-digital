— Questions

Frequently Asked

Does the voice agent sound human?

Yes — modern voice models plus carefully tuned prompts make calls sound natural. Most callers cannot tell it is AI unless you tell them.

Will it work with my existing phone number?

Yes — we connect through Twilio or port your current number, then wire the agent into your calendar and CRM.

Can it handle both French and English?

Yes — the agent detects the caller's language and can switch mid-call, using Quebec French (fr-CA) and English (en-CA).

What happens if the agent cannot answer?

It transfers the call to a human or takes a message, and logs the call in your CRM — no caller is ever left hanging.

Are you compliant with Quebec's Law 25?

Yes — the agent discloses it is automated, collects only what is needed, and we document the data handling for your privacy lead.

What does it cost to run?

A one-time build plus a monthly care plan from $349/mo covering monitoring, tweaks and usage guidance. Pause anytime.
